
$∇f_j = \left[  \frac{∂}{∂x}  i + \frac{∂}{∂y}  j + k \frac{∂}{∂z}  \right] f$

En coordenadas polar cilíndricas :

$∇f_j = \frac{∂f_j}{∂r} i_r + \frac{1}{r} \frac{∂f_j}{∂θ} i_θ + \frac{∂f_j}{∂z} k$

y en coordenadas polar esféricas :

$∇f_j = \frac{∂f_j}{∂r} i_r + \frac{1}{r} \frac{∂f_j}{∂θ} i_θ + \frac{1}{r sinθ} \frac{∂f_j}{∂φ} i_φ + \frac{1}{r} \frac{∂f_j}{∂θ} k$
